Where every eMAX field comes from

When a customer pays for an RHP warranty on the microsite, it is posted to the eMAX accounting system as a Cash Sale plus a payment receipt. This page lists every field we send, where its value comes from, and an example.

1
Customer paysFiuu webhook marks the warranty Paid
2
Customer checkGetCust confirms the cash customer exists
3
Cash SaleImportSales → eMAX returns a CS number
4
Payment receiptImportPayment settles that CS → OR number
Posted once, retried by handPosting runs once, automatically, after payment. If it fails, an alert email is sent and the warranty stays Failed until someone checks eMAX and retries with POST api/emax/post/{warrantyId}. Nothing is retried automatically.

1 · Cash Sale

POST{EmaxWebAPIUrl}/Sales?Method=ImportSales

Sent as a JSON array with one header. Before anything is sent, the post fails if any of these settings is blank: EmaxBranchCode, EmaxCustomerCode, EmaxItemCode_{plan} (or EmaxItemCode), EmaxPaymentType, EmaxPaymentAccountCode.

The whole warranty is posted as one line, not one line per tyre. The tyres are summarised in the line's Remark, which eMAX prints under the description.

PricingThe line is priced from what the customer paid, not from the tyre prices in RHP_Warranty_Reg_Detail.Price. When 3 tyres are billed as 4 × the unit price, that billed amount is what appears here.

4 · Cash Sale PDF

GET{EmaxWebAPIUrl}/AppWA/Sales/GetSalesPdf

Called when a user clicks Cash Sale on the Status page. It is a GET that carries a JSON body. eMAX returns a print link; the microsite downloads the PDF and shows it in the browser.

FieldSourceExample

AppWA token claims

Built and encrypted by the microsite itself (signed HS256, then encrypted A256KW / A256CBC-HS512), valid for 1 hour. It is never sent to the browser.
