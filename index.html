Where every eMAX field comes from

When a customer pays for an RHP warranty on the microsite, it is posted to eMAX as a Cash Sale and a payment receipt. Every field eMAX receives is listed below with its source and an example. The example is warranty RHP-20261105-E94325329: 4 tyres, paid RM 108.00 including 8% SST.

How posting works

1
Payment recordedFiuu confirms the payment and the warranty becomes Paid
2
Customer checkeMAX confirms the cash customer exists
3
Cash SaleeMAX creates the sale and returns its CS number
4
Payment receipteMAX records the receipt against that CS and returns its OR number
i
Posting runs once, right after payment. If it fails, an alert email is sent and nothing is retried automatically. Check eMAX first, then retry with POST api/emax/post/{warrantyId}.

Where values come from

Each source in the tables carries one of these four badges.

Setting
A row in Master_SystemInformation. The table shows the parameter name.
Database
A column of a microsite table:
  • Warranty: RHP_Warranty_Reg_Header
  • Tyres: RHP_Warranty_Reg_Detail
  • Payment: RHP_Payment
  • Fiuu log: Fiuu_Payment_Log
  • Post log: Emax_Post_Log
Calculated
Worked out by the microsite. See Shared values.
Fixed
Always the same value. Use Hide fixed in the top bar to hide these rows.

Shared values

Worked out once and reused by several fields.

Open questions for eMAX (checked on CS1007 and CS1008, 06/11/2026):
  • Vehicle prints ProjectNo, but eMAX only accepts an existing Project code. Free text such as TEH1234 MAZDA CX-8 fails with ProjectName Not Found, so we send null. Which field takes the vehicle as free text?
  • Outlet Details at the top stays empty. The outlet block in InvoiceRemark prints at the bottom instead, after eMAX's own PAID / BALANCE text. Which field fills Outlet Details? Maybe DeliveryRemark; not tested.
  • Address stays empty because Address1 to Address3 are null. Should the consumer's address go there?

eMAX fieldSourceExample

One line per tyre. What the customer paid is split across the lines in proportion to each tyre's RHP_Warranty_Reg_Detail.Price, with any rounding cent on the last line, so the lines always add up to the amount paid. When 3 tyres are billed as 4 × the unit price, each of the 3 lines carries a third of that total.
